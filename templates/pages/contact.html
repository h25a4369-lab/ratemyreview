Contact us

Need help with your account, spotted a problem, or want a place added? Get in touch.

Email

The quickest way to reach the team is by email at hello@ratemyreview.example. We aim to reply within two working days.

Reporting a review

If a specific review breaks our guidelines, use the Report this review link underneath it while logged in. Reports go straight to the administrator queue with the review attached, which is much faster than emailing a screenshot.

Requesting a new place

Send the place name, its location and a one-line description. An administrator will add it, after which it can be searched and reviewed like any other place.

Account problems

Include the email address you registered with and a short description of what happened. Never send your password — nobody on the team will ever ask for it.

Response times

  • Account help — 1–2 working days
  • Content reports — reviewed in order received
  • New place requests — usually within a week

Before you write

Our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy explain what we store and how reviews are moderated.
